## 1. Positioning (say the same thing everywhere)

- **One line:** Every laptop deserves the same security as the office.
- **What it is:** One lightweight agent that puts company policy on the laptop itself: web gateway, DLP, app and VPN control, malware scanning, device posture. macOS, Windows, Linux. Any network.
- **Why now:** Work left the office. Security didn't.
- **Who it's for:** IT heads and founders of 20–500 person companies with laptops outside the office.
- **Tone:** Calm, precise, confident. Black, white, one orange. Short sentences. No fear-mongering, no stock photos of hoodies.
- **Tagline:** Secure today. Enable tomorrow.
- **Hashtag:** #Aavishield on everything. Category tags: #EndpointSecurity #DLP #ZeroTrust.

## 6. Make it feel big (the "vibe" playbook)

- **Same image everywhere on the same day.** When a buyer sees the Readout on LinkedIn at 9, X at 10 and Instagram at 11, it reads as a campaign, not a post.
- **Founder first, page second.** The company page reposts the founder, not the other way round.
- **Comment seeding.** Three teammates comment a real question on every post within 10 minutes. The algorithm reads early comments as signal.
- **Employee advocacy.** Everyone on the team changes their LinkedIn banner to the Aavishield banner on T-20 and posts once in their own words during week 3.
- **Countdown stories daily from T-7.** Edit the number in the Story / Countdown frame.
- **Numbers, not adjectives.** "214 on the waitlist", "38 laptops enrolled in an afternoon". Post them.
- **One orange moment.** The avatar switches to orange on launch night and stays orange for the launch week. People notice.
- **Reply to everything** for the first 72 hours. Reach follows replies.
- **Retarget.** From T-0, run a small Meta + LinkedIn retargeting ad (₹500–1000/day) showing Post 01 to everyone who visited the site in the last 30 days.

## 7. Measure

Weekly, every Sunday: waitlist sign-ups (primary), profile visits, follower growth, saves and reshares (LinkedIn/IG), link clicks, email open rate. After launch add: sign-ups, demos booked, devices enrolled.
Targets to aim for by T-0: 300 waitlist sign-ups, 1,000 LinkedIn followers, 500 Instagram followers. If week 1 is under 25% of pace, shift budget to founder DMs and the retargeting ad early.

## 8. Risks

- Launch slips: keep the date off the images except banners (they say 16 Nov; re-export if the date moves).
- Founder bandwidth: the founder posts are the highest-leverage items. If anything is cut, cut company-page posts, not founder posts.
- Fonts: all Figma frames use Satoshi, Inter, DM Mono and Sora. Export from Figma, don't rebuild in Canva.
