Data Loss Prevention shows you what sensitive data leaves your devices and where it went.

## Monitor-only by design

DLP records findings and **never blocks**. Security gets visibility without breaking the way people work. You decide what to do with a finding.

## Which destinations are covered

Any website or desktop app that sends data over the network, including:

- Gmail and Outlook
- Microsoft Teams and OneDrive
- WhatsApp Web
- ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini

## How content is inspected

1. Uploads and messages are inspected on the device as they leave.
2. Documents, archives, PDFs and images are unpacked.
3. Scanned or photographed pages are read with **OCR**.
4. Content is checked against detectors and classified with AI.

See [What DLP detects](/knowledge-hub/what-dlp-detects) for the full list.

## What is stored

We keep the finding and a short masked preview, **not a copy of the file**.
