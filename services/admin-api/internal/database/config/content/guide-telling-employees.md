Security tools work best when people understand them. Aavishield is built to be visible, not hidden.

## Employees install it themselves

Each employee installs the agent from their own portal, so nobody finds it on their laptop by surprise.

## What employees can see

The employee portal shows people their devices and what was blocked.

## What you must tell them

Our [acceptable use policy](/acceptable-use-policy) requires you to tell employees what is monitored. Cover at least:

- which controls are on, for example web filtering and DLP,
- whether their device has activity monitoring switched on,
- who can see the data, and
- how long it is kept.

## A suggested message

> We are rolling out Aavishield on company laptops to protect customer and company data. It blocks risky sites and apps, and records when sensitive data leaves a device. It does not block your normal work. You will install it yourself from the employee portal.
