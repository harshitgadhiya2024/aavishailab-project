A personal VPN is the easiest way around web filtering. Aavishield closes that gap by default.

## What is blocked

- Known VPN desktop clients
- Downloads of VPN installers
- Proxy sites
- Known VPN and proxy browser extensions

## Is there anything to configure?

No. VPN Control is on from the moment a device enrolls.

## What about our company VPN?

If your company uses an approved VPN, talk to us during the demo about allowing it for your setup.
