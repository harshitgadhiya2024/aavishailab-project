Before any device is protected, Aavishield needs to know your company and the people in it. This takes a few minutes.

## 1. Register your company

Sign up, verify your email address and set up two-factor sign-in. Every admin sign-in needs a second factor: an emailed one-time code or an authenticator app.

After signing in you land on the overview, which tells you what to do next.

## 2. Add employees

You can add employees in two ways:

1. **Import a CSV** when you have more than a handful of people.
2. **Add them one by one** for small teams or new joiners.

## 3. Group employees into teams

Put people into teams as you add them. Policy can later be targeted at the whole organisation, a team, or a single employee, so a little structure now saves work later.

> **Tip:** Create a team for IT first. Their devices make the best first pilot group.

## What happens next

Once employees exist, each one can sign in to the employee portal and install the agent. See [Install the agent](/knowledge-hub/install-the-agent).
