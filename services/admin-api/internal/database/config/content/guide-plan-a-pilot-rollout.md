The technology is the easy part. Most of a rollout is communication, so plan for it.

## Step 1: Pilot

Enroll five to ten devices, including IT's own. Turn on DLP and the obvious web categories. A pilot can be live in an afternoon.

## Step 2: Review real findings

Look at what the pilot actually produces. Talk to the teams most affected before tightening anything. Because DLP is monitor-only, nothing you learn here has interrupted anyone's work.

## Step 3: Tell employees

Explain what Aavishield does and why. Share your acceptable use policy, which must tell people what is monitored. Employees install the agent themselves, so nobody is surprised.

## Step 4: Roll out

Send the enrollment link to everyone and track coverage on the overview. Most companies reach every device within two to three weeks, and most of that time goes on talking to employees.

## Checklist

- Pilot group chosen, including IT
- DLP and core web categories switched on
- Findings reviewed with the affected teams
- Acceptable use policy shared
- Enrollment link sent to everyone
- Coverage checked on the overview
