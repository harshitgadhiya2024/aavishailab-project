## How pricing works

Aavishield is priced **per enrolled device** and billed annually, in Indian rupees plus 18% GST for customers in India (with a GST invoice), and in US dollars everywhere else. Count every laptop and desktop that will run the agent, whether Mac, Windows or Linux.

## Which plan fits

| Plan | Devices | India, per device per year (+ GST) | Outside India, per device per year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essentials | 10 to 50 | ₹1,799 | $39 |
| Business | 10 to 50 | ₹3,599 | $79 |
| Enterprise | More than 50 | From ₹5,499 | From $119 |

The minimum is 10 devices. Monthly billing is available for about 20% more. Fleets of more than 100 devices get volume pricing.

Use the device picker on the [pricing page](/pricing) to see which plan fits, and an estimate of your yearly cost including GST.

## Start with a pilot

Every plan starts with a free 30-day pilot on up to 10 devices. No card is needed. See [Plan a pilot rollout](/knowledge-hub/plan-a-pilot-rollout).

## Cancelling

Cancellation stops renewal, and the service continues to the end of the paid term. See our [refund and cancellation policy](/refund-and-cancellation).
